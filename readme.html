hobbyutil

This is a collection of a few files I've written over the years, usually about some topic that has interested me or resulted in an experiment. The python scripts that used to be here are at plib. All this stuff is now under the MIT license.

Electrical

BNC_connector_power.pdf Experimental data about using RF coax cables with BNC connectors for DC and low-frequency power.

CurrentSource.pdf How to make a battery-operated 1 ampere current source used to make low resistance measurements.

MeasuringESR.pdf Describes a technique of estimating a capacitor's ESR (equivalent series resistance) without having to buy a special meter.

fuse_as_shunt.pdf Notes on using ATO automobile fuses as poor-man's shunts.

hppn.zip This is a compilation of various 8-digit HP part numbers translated into conventional industry part numbers. This list may be of use to those with old HP instruments that need to find a replacement part.

ind.zip Provides an Open Office spreadsheet that can calculate the inductance of common electrical structures. Includes a PDF document describing the use and which gives references for the formulas used.

logic_probe.pdf Discusses the use of a logic probe.

octopus.pdf Build an Octopus, a handy electrical troubleshooting tool (you need an oscilloscope).

PartsStorageMethods.pdf Describes one way of storing lots of little electronic parts and how to find them quickly.

RMS.pdf An article for hobbyists about making RMS electrical measurements.

PortableVoltageStandard.pdf Simple voltage standard you can make with one resistor, an IC that costs about a buck, and three AA batteries.

Math

AnalyticGeometry.pdf Contains formulas relating to analytic geometry and other math stuff I need to look up on a regular basis.

Concise300.pdf Discusses the Concise 300, a circular slide rule still in production in Japan.

ElementaryFunctions.pdf Graphs of a variety of elementary math functions, useful for a quick picture of how they behave or to grab one or two significant figures of the value.

scale.zip The scale.pdf file contains two sheets of paper with slide rule type scales on them. You may find it useful for simple technical calculations.

TrigDegrees.pdf Gives some algebraic expressions for a few special values of trigonometric functions in degrees.

triguc.zip Contains a vector drawing of the trig functions on the unit circle. The python script used to generate the graphics is included, so you can tweak it to your tastes.

Miscellaneous

antifreeze.pdf How to calculate how much antifreeze to add to an existing partially-filled radiator to get a desired concentration. Also looks at the refractometer.

Donor.pdf Why I have organ donor checked on my driver's license.

fountain_pen_primer.pdf Discusses the care and feeding of fountain pens as writing tools.

Markup.pdf Derives the equations for markup and profit used in business.

paper_sizes.pdf Shows a scale drawing of various ISO and US paper sizes.

pqs.zip Python scripts to simulate a production process that is inspected by a measurement process with a significant measurement uncertainty.

shaving.pdf Some thoughts on shaving your beard.

shoulder_surgery.html A document describing my experiences with shoulder surgeries to help others understand some of the things one goes through. A shoulder injury is one of the more inconvenient injuries because it gets in the way of so many of life's activities.

XmasTomatoes.pdf Using Christmas tree lights to keep tomato plants from freezing at night.

Science

chemical_names.pdf A list of archaic chemical names with their modern equivalents and chemical formulas.

diurnal_variations.pdf Shows a plot of the light from the sky measured with a cheap photodiode. This is a simple experiment that would be fun do to with a child.

elements.zip Contains elements.pdf, a document that contains a periodic table of the elements, a plot of the vapor pressures of the elements, values of physical parameters sorted by value, and various physical parameters of the elements plotted as a function of atomic number.

SolarSystemScaleModel.pdf Python script that prints out the dimensions of a scaled solar system. You can use it to make a scale solar system in your yard or on your street.

SphericalShell.pdf Discusses gravitation and electrostatics inside a uniform spherical shell and why there is no force on a particle. Also looks at Henry Cavendish's elegant experiment in the 1700's showing that the exponent in Coulomb's Law is 2.

GNU_units.pdf A short blurb on the capabilities of the useful GNU units program.

Shop

bucket.zip Shows how to calculate bucket volumes and mark volume calibration marks on nearly any bucket. Includes a python script that will do the calculations for you.

Calipers.pdf Discussion and use of old-style machinist calipers.

CartPlatform.pdf Simple platform for Harbor Freight garden cart.

MachinistClamp.pdf Discusses machinist's parallel clamps, why they're useful, and how to make your own.

cove.zip Python script shows you how to cut a cove with your table saw. Use this formula and method when it just has to be done correctly on a workpiece you can't mess up on.

Demagnetizer.pdf A simple demagnetizing tool made from scrap materials.

DitchPump_pub.pdf Comments and tips on using a ditch pump to water your lawn.

LayingOutFrustumWithDividers.pdf Shows how to lay out the frustum of a cone with dividers in your shop.

GlendaGuard.pdf Describes a simple concrete sprinkler guard that my wife designed and built.

hammer.pdf Discusses the common hammer types and making a new handle for one.

HoseFitting.pdf Here's an effective way to secure a hose to a hose fitting. It's better than anything I've found in a store.

LittleVise.pdf Describes a small vise made from 1 inch square bar stock. It slips into a pocket and is handy for small tasks around the shop and home.

nozzle.pdf Nice hose nozzle you can make if you have a lathe.

pipes.pdf Derivation of a formula that can be used to make a template for cutting the end of a pipe so that it can be welded to another pipe.

PullingFencePosts.pdf Using a class 2 lever can be a surprisingly effective way to pull fence posts out of the ground.

SawBuck.pdf A simple sawbuck that's made from eight identical pieces of 2x4.

sine_sticks.pdf How to build a simple device from scrap that will measure angles in the shop.

weigh.pdf Demonstrates how I weighed our trailer with a lever. With a 12 foot long 4x4, I was able to measure 2500 pounds.

YankeePushDrill.pdf Discusses the Yankee screwdriver, a useful tool that has been in production for more than 100 years.
