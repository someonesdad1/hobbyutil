hobbyutil
=========

This is a collection of a few files I've written over the years, usually about some
topic that has interested me or resulted in an experiment.  The python scripts that used
to be here are at [plib](https://github.com/someonesdad1/plib).  All this stuff is now
under the MIT license.

Electrical
==========

[BNC_connector_power.pdf](elec/BNC_connector_power.pdf) Experimental data about using RF
coax cables with BNC connectors for DC and low-frequency power.<p>

[CurrentSource.pdf](elec/CurrentSource.pdf) How to make a battery-operated 1 ampere
current source used to make low resistance measurements.<p>

[MeasuringESR.pdf](elec/MeasuringESR.pdf) Describes a technique of estimating a
capacitor's ESR (equivalent series resistance) without having to buy a special meter.<p>

[fuse_as_shunt.pdf](elec/fuse_as_shunt.pdf) Notes on using ATO automobile fuses as
poor-man's shunts.<p>

[hppn.zip](elec/hppn.zip) This is a compilation of various 8-digit HP part numbers
translated into conventional industry part numbers.  This list may be of use to those
with old HP instruments that need to find a replacement part.<p>

[ind.zip](elec/ind.zip) Provides an Open Office spreadsheet that can calculate the
inductance of common electrical structures.  Includes a PDF document describing the use
and which gives references for the formulas used.<p>

[logic_probe.pdf](elec/logic_probe.pdf) Discusses the use of a logic probe.<p>

[octopus.pdf](elec/octopus.pdf) Build an Octopus, a handy electrical troubleshooting
tool (you need an oscilloscope).<p>

[PartsStorageMethods.pdf](elec/PartsStorageMethods.pdf) Describes one way of storing
lots of little electronic parts and how to find them quickly.<p>

[RMS.pdf](elec/RMS.pdf) An article for hobbyists about making RMS electrical
measurements.<p>

[PortableVoltageStandard.pdf](elec/PortableVoltageStandard.pdf) Simple voltage standard
you can make with one resistor, an IC that costs about a buck, and three AA
batteries.<p>

Math
====

[AnalyticGeometry.pdf](math/AnalyticGeometry.pdf) Contains formulas relating to analytic
geometry and other math stuff I need to look up on a regular basis.<p>

[Concise300.pdf](math/Concise300.pdf) Discusses the Concise 300, a circular slide rule
still in production in Japan.<p>

[ElementaryFunctions.pdf](math/ElementaryFunctions.pdf) Graphs of a variety of
elementary math functions, useful for a quick picture of how they behave or to grab one
or two significant figures of the value.<p>

[scale.zip](math/scale.zip) The scale.pdf file contains two sheets of paper with slide
rule type scales on them. You may find it useful for simple technical calculations.<p>

[TrigDegrees.pdf](math/TrigDegrees.pdf) Gives some algebraic expressions for a few
special values of trigonometric functions in degrees.<p>

[triguc.zip](math/triguc.zip) Contains a vector drawing of the trig functions on the
unit circle.  The python script used to generate the graphics is included, so you can
tweak it to your tastes.<p>

Miscellaneous
=============

[antifreeze.pdf](eng/antifreeze.pdf) How to calculate how much antifreeze to add to an
existing partially-filled radiator to get a desired concentration.  Also looks at the
refractometer.<p>

[Donor.pdf](misc/Donor.pdf) Why I have organ donor checked on my driver's license.<p>

[fountain_pen_primer.pdf](misc/fountain_pen_primer.pdf) Discusses the care and feeding
of fountain pens as writing tools.<p>

[Markup.pdf](misc/Markup.pdf) Derives the equations for markup and profit used in
business.<p>

[paper_sizes.pdf](misc/paper_sizes.pdf) Shows a scale drawing of various ISO and US
paper sizes.<p>

[pqs.zip](eng/pqs.zip) Python scripts to simulate a production process that is inspected
by a measurement process with a significant measurement uncertainty.<p>

[shaving.pdf](misc/shaving.pdf) Some thoughts on shaving your beard.<p>

[shoulder_surgery.html](misc/shoulder_surgery.html) A document describing my experiences
with shoulder surgeries to help others understand some of the things one goes through.
A shoulder injury is one of the more inconvenient injuries because it gets in the way of
so many of life's activities.<p>

[XmasTomatoes.pdf](misc/XmasTomatoes.pdf) Using Christmas tree lights to keep tomato
plants from freezing at night.<p>

Science
=======

[chemical_names.pdf](science/chemical_names.pdf) A list of archaic chemical names with
their modern equivalents and chemical formulas.<p>

[diurnal_variations.pdf](science/diurnal_variations.pdf) Shows a plot of the light from
the sky measured with a cheap photodiode.  This is a simple experiment that would be fun
do to with a child.<p>

[elements.zip](science/elements.zip) Contains elements.pdf, a document that contains a
periodic table of the elements, a plot of the vapor pressures of the elements, values of
physical parameters sorted by value, and various physical parameters of the elements
plotted as a function of atomic number.<p>

[SolarSystemScaleModel.pdf](science/SolarSystemScaleModel.pdf) Python script that prints
out the dimensions of a scaled solar system.  You can use it to make a scale solar
system in your yard or on your street.<p>

[SphericalShell.pdf](science/SphericalShell.pdf) Discusses gravitation and
electrostatics inside a uniform spherical shell and why there is no force on a particle.
Also looks at Henry Cavendish's elegant experiment in the 1700's showing that the
exponent in Coulomb's Law is 2.<p>

[GNU_units.pdf](science/GNU_units.pdf) A short blurb on the capabilities of the useful
GNU units program.<p>

Shop
====

[bucket.zip](shop/bucket.zip) Shows how to calculate bucket volumes and mark volume
calibration marks on nearly any bucket.  Includes a python script that will do the
calculations for you.<p>

[Calipers.pdf](shop/Calipers.pdf) Discussion and use of old-style machinist calipers.<p>

[CartPlatform.pdf](shop/CartPlatform.pdf) Simple platform for Harbor Freight garden
cart.<p>

[MachinistClamp.pdf](shop/MachinistClamp.pdf) Discusses machinist's parallel clamps, why
they're useful, and how to make your own.<p>

[cove.zip](shop/cove.zip) Python script shows you how to cut a cove with your table saw.
Use this formula and method when it just has to be done correctly on a workpiece you
can't mess up on.<p>

[Demagnetizer.pdf](shop/Demagnetizer.pdf) A simple demagnetizing tool made from scrap
materials.<p>

[DitchPump_pub.pdf](shop/DitchPump_pub.pdf) Comments and tips on using a ditch pump to
water your lawn.<p>

[LayingOutFrustumWithDividers.pdf](shop/LayingOutFrustumWithDividers.pdf) Shows how to
lay out the frustum of a cone with dividers in your shop.<p>

[GlendaGuard.pdf](shop/GlendaGuard.pdf) Describes a simple concrete sprinkler guard that
my wife designed and built.<p>

[hammer.pdf](shop/hammer.pdf) Discusses the common hammer types and making a new handle
for one.<p>

[HoseFitting.pdf](shop/HoseFitting.pdf) Here's an effective way to secure a hose to a
hose fitting.  It's better than anything I've found in a store.<p>

[LittleVise.pdf](shop/LittleVise.pdf) Describes a small vise made from 1 inch square bar
stock.  It slips into a pocket and is handy for small tasks around the shop and home.<p>

[nozzle.pdf](shop/nozzle.pdf) Nice hose nozzle you can make if you have a lathe.<p>

[pipes.pdf](shop/pipes.pdf) Derivation of a formula that can be used to make a template
for cutting the end of a pipe so that it can be welded to another pipe.<p>

[PullingFencePosts.pdf](shop/PullingFencePosts.pdf) Using a class 2 lever can be a
surprisingly effective way to pull fence posts out of the ground.<p>

[SawBuck.pdf](shop/SawBuck.pdf) A simple sawbuck that's made from eight identical pieces
of 2x4.<p>

[sine_sticks.pdf](shop/sine_sticks.pdf) How to build a simple device from scrap that
will measure angles in the shop.<p>

[weigh.pdf](shop/weigh.pdf) Demonstrates how I weighed our trailer with a lever. With a
12 foot long 4x4, I was able to measure 2500 pounds.<p>

[YankeePushDrill.pdf](shop/YankeePushDrill.pdf) Discusses the Yankee screwdriver, a
useful tool that has been in production for more than 100 years.<p>
